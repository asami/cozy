# Phase 27: SmartDox Site Media Registration

Status: closed

Start date: 2026-08-11
Close date: 2026-08-11

Dependency: closed Phase 26 article-media publication and BoK integration
contracts, including its exact-locale registry semantics and accepted media
publication evidence. This phase does not turn `simplemodeling.org` into a
Cozy BoK or require a public/non-SNAPSHOT SmartDox release as a start gate.

## Goal

Let the special `simplemodeling.org` BoK register media for SmartDox site
articles from a normal Cozy media package. The site is built directly by
SmartDox rather than through a Cozy BoK build. Article sources continue to be
registered and discovered by SmartDox normally; infographics generated by
skills and videos generated and published by Cozy must additionally be
registered into SmartDox through supported product commands.

Cozy owns normal-media-package orchestration because it knows `media.yaml`
resources, public paths, and accepted video publication evidence. SmartDox
owns the provider-neutral article-media publication schema/model, validation,
and site projection. SmartDox/Dox may provide a generic safe registry-writer
API, but Cozy remains the primary user-facing orchestration boundary.

## Scope

In scope:

- contract promotion and responsibility pinning for site-media registration;
- explicit article binding independent of a media package identifier;
- exact-locale infographic and externally published video registration from a
  normal media package;
- deterministic role-local registry merge with locking, preflight, and atomic
  replacement;
- supported skill orchestration and Part 5 end-to-end acceptance; and
- documentation, validation, review, and closure of the registration boundary.

Out of scope:

- `cozy bok publish-media`, BoK scan/build/repository/staging dependencies, or
  turning `simplemodeling.org` into a Cozy BoK;
- arbitrary target, generated-site, or repository scans;
- skill or manual mutation of a SmartDox registry;
- media generation, upload, or site deployment in the registration command;
- implicit host fallback or article binding inferred from a path, filename, or
  generated site; and
- coupled deployment or unrelated SmartDox/Cozy internals in a registry
  record.

The normative descriptor, command, validation, registry, and workflow contract
is `docs/spec/smartdox-site-media-registration.md`; its responsibility boundary
is `docs/design/smartdox-site-media-registration.md`.

## Registration Boundary

The registration record remains provider-neutral and reuses Phase 26
`article-media-publication` semantics:

- records live at `metadata/article-media/<articleIdentity>.json`;
- article identity is explicitly supplied because `knowledge.id` and article
  identity may differ;
- locale is exact and canonical, with no fallback;
- owner bundle and role/locale updates merge deterministically while
  preserving unrelated entries;
- registry mutation uses lock/preflight/atomic replacement semantics;
- infographics carry their public path;
- externally hosted videos carry their provider, published state, and
  `watch_url`; and
- SmartDox records contain no Cozy internals.

Registration consumes accepted production evidence from the normal package and
does not generate or upload media. The registration command must not infer an
article from a package path, filename, or generated site.
