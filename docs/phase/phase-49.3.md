# Phase 49.3: Presentation Semantics Currentness and Operational Driver

Status: IN PROGRESS

Plan date: 2026-09-09
Split from: [Phase 49](phase-49.md)
Depends on: Phase 49.2

## Goal

Complete the normal Document Project operational path by propagating exact
identity-driven stale/currentness through presentation semantics, dependent
semantic projections, and public confirmation, then prove the path with an
Article-9-shaped local driver. The driver is local acceptance only; it does not
claim editorial completion, publication, deployment, upload, push, or any
external mutation.

confirmation identity/receipt behavior. It closes the original DEV-018

## Closure criteria

- Exact identity changes deterministically stale all required dependent
  authorities and public confirmation without automatic write-back.
- Receipt currentness remains distinct from strict semantic completeness and
  Phase-46.1 coverage verification.
- An Article-9-shaped local driver exercises the normal public route through
  recovery, without claiming Article 9 publication or external mutation.
- Focused executable specifications, review, full Cozy validation, and release
  closure complete the final DEV-018 workflow-integration child.

## Non-goals

- Reopening the accepted Phase-49 state/grammar, Phase-49.1 surfaces, or
  Phase-49.2 public adapter.
- New semantic schema, permissive validator, renderer, or receipt model.
- Article 9 editorial completion, publication, deployment, registration,
  upload, push, or external mutation.
