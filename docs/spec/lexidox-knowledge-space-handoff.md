# LexiDox Knowledge Space handoff

Status: adopted v1 design baseline; built producer and P93-01 presentation mapping pending. The authoritative full wire schema and diagnostics are the SmartDox [v1 specification](https://github.com/asami/smartdox/blob/7207fa191cd32bd383684ca4b278ff402ddbc096/docs/spec/lexidox-knowledge-space.md); the [decision record](../design/lexidox-producer-consumer-boundary.md) records revisions, ownership and delivery gates.

## Source and sections

V1 opts in through `HEAD.lexidox`: `version = 1`, `profile = "simplemodeling-v1"`, `space`, `term_iri`, `concept_iri`, and `page_iri` are required for glossary pages. IRIs are explicit absolute HTTP(S), supplied by author or deployment configuration. `concept_type` and `knowledge_form` are independent optional lowercase identifiers. Legacy `term_type` aliases the classification; differing normalized values with `concept_type` are an error. No form means ordinary rules; no classification remains absent. A reference-bearing document may declare version and optional Space without being a glossary page.

The profile admits DEFINITION, SOURCE, CONTEXT, RELATIONS, SIMPLEMODELING, EXTENSION, CONSTRAINTS and REFERENCES; Pattern additionally admits PROBLEM, SOLUTION and CONSEQUENCES. Each standard section occurs at most once and has nonempty content. Ordinary requires DEFINITION; Pattern requires DEFINITION, CONTEXT, PROBLEM and SOLUTION, and missing CONSEQUENCES warns. Other admitted sections are optional and order is unrestricted. SOURCE is attribution and REFERENCES supporting reading; CONTEXT is Space applicability and SIMPLEMODELING a methodology connection. Other BoKs register their own versioned profiles rather than changing parser semantics.

An all-uppercase heading with a letter attempts a standard section; an unknown name is a source-located error. Lowercase, mixed-case and Japanese headings are free and preserve source order, body, links and locale. Exactly `SimpleModeling` is the legacy spelling of SIMPLEMODELING; its original heading is retained. Existing SmartDox grammar, DEFINITION, RELATIONS, aliases and legacy non-v1 behavior remain in force. Examples must retain a blank line after each heading.

## Identity and references

A case-sensitive Space ID follows `[a-z0-9]+(?:-[a-z0-9]+)*`; a one-to-one registry supplies its absolute Space IRI. Term, Concept, page and source-document IRIs are separate supplied identities and remain stable across moves. Page IRIs are unique and a `(Space, Term IRI)` has at most one page. Equal labels do not imply identity: separate Spaces may use different Concepts or explicitly share one Concept while retaining separate pages. No identity is inferred from directory or category.

`<lexiref term="label" space="id">visible text</lexiref>` selects explicit Space, else document Space, else all Spaces. Exact title/alias matching follows NFC normalization and trimming, case-sensitive and locale-independent. One page resolves; zero gives undefined and multiple give ambiguity with source span and all candidate Space, Term, Concept and page identities. There is no wider-scope retry. Existing explicit `<term ref>` behavior remains separate. Label selection happens after resolution and cannot change identity or target.

## Interchange and graph

The producer emits the UTF-8 `lexidox-knowledge/1` envelope with ordered `spaces`, `pages`, `references` and `diagnostics`. Pages carry profile, Space/Term/Concept/page/source identities, titles, aliases, optional classification/form, ordered sections and ordered metadata properties. Sections retain original heading, SmartDox source fragment, textual projection and span. Metadata uses ordered properties and typed values, retaining repeated names. The producer retains unknown optional fields on round trip; consumers may ignore them in a known version and reject an unsupported major version. Failed references have diagnostic indexes and no resolved identities. The authoritative schema specifies all fields and cardinalities.

The v1 graph uses distinct `lx:KnowledgeSpace`, `lx:Term`, `skos:Concept` and `lx:TermPage` resources. A page links its Space, Term, Concept and source using `lx:inSpace`, `lx:term`, `lx:concept` and `prov:wasDerivedFrom`; titles/aliases use SKOS labels. Classification and form are literals. Each ordered section has a deterministic resource, name, text, source provenance and source-position literals. SOURCE, EXTENSION and CONSTRAINTS remain distinct section content, rather than inferred assertions. RDF and JSON-LD serialize the same graph; existing occurrence projection remains intact. The producer specification defines the section IRI algorithm and reserved namespace.

## Delivery and compatibility

Without v1 opt-in, existing behavior remains unchanged. V1 preserves legacy syntax and explicit RDF terminology, and can retain an explicitly supplied old path-shaped Concept IRI; migration supplies the remaining identities. SmartDox owns the implementation, diagnostics, schema and producer fixtures. Its contract revision is `7207fa191cd32bd383684ca4b278ff402ddbc096`; it is not a built producer. Before P92-02, obtain its artifact coordinate and source revision, actual Japanese/English, legacy and multi-Space source fixtures and outputs, and producer validation receipts. Cozy consumes the actual output through its existing BoK path and declares available resources to SIE; it does not reparse or reconstruct missing producer output.

The portable fixture family in the producer plan covers separate and shared concepts, reference scope and failures, process plus Pattern, free and legacy sections, identity and wire validation, and a later presentation extension. Phase 92 validates knowledge admission and projections; Phase 93 adds typed optional labels and preferences, fixes precedence and missing-label behavior in P93-01, and validates actual PDF/HTML selection and switching. The same semantic fixtures and identities must survive the extension. Neither the fixtures nor a built v1 delivery are yet received.
