## Cozy Boundary Fixes

The real Overview builds exposed four boundary defects that are now covered by
executable specifications:

1. Effective render duration now includes lead silence, actual audio duration,
   and tail silence. Authored target duration remains unchanged in metadata,
   while longer narration extends the rendered scene rather than being cut.
2. Docker Remotion output is written to a project-local staging path, validated,
   and then copied to the configured part output. This supports official output
   paths outside the Remotion project root without relying on an unmounted host
   path inside the container.
3. Docker ffmpeg assembly stages every part and the final render under the
   project root. ffprobe validates the staged final video before Cozy copies it
   to the configured publication path.
4. Silent scenes generated by Cozy no longer claim narration-provider,
   execution-mode, voice, or model provenance. Credit resolution therefore
   evaluates only voices that were actually synthesized, and a required voice
   credit cannot be triggered by the default voice of a silent title or final
   scene.

The Japanese package review also found that its credit selectors omitted the
VOICEVOX style suffix while synthesis records canonical identities such as
`四国めたん/ノーマル`. The source-managed profile now matches the authoritative
manifest identities exactly instead of relying on ambiguous provider-specific
matching.
