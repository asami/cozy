# Phase 58.2 Interactive Confirmation Projection Decision

Date: 2026-09-12

## Trigger

The Phase 58.1 Document and Summary review HTMLs were exercised with the real
SimpleModeling.org Article 9 inputs. Both source DSLs were useful, but the
rendered document remained close to a final article and the rendered summary
remained a list of heading/message cards. Neither page made the relationship
among recursive Core meaning, prose realization, summary selection, and
editorial omission sufficiently visible.

A manual design exercise produced two user-approved Article 9 reference HTMLs:

- a Document confirmation workspace joining a selectable Core tree, selected
  Step Structure, and highlighted prose realization; and
- a Summary confirmation workspace joining unit flow, one slide-level semantic
  explanation, exact sources, retained points, and deliberate omissions.

Review also confirmed that the existing projection contains Article 9-specific
Japanese labels in Cozy Scala and that the v1 Summary Description does not own
enough information to reproduce the approved summary review without renderer
inference.

## Decision

Create **Phase 58.2: Interactive Document and Summary Confirmation
Projection** as a successor to completed Phase 58.1.

The manual HTMLs are accepted as UX and behavior exemplars. They are not
semantic authorities and Cozy output is not required to match their bytes.
Phase 58.2 will express the missing localized and summary-review semantics in
strict v2 authoring DSLs, then generate functionally and visually equivalent
self-contained confirmation HTMLs from the real Article 9 inputs.

The generated projections must preserve three different Core relationships:

1. recursive Step containment;
2. typed Flow among direct child Steps; and
3. Step-local Structure and typed Relations.

No renderer may synthesize an unrecorded semantic Relation. Prototype arrows
must be reconciled with Core before acceptance. A visible relation is admitted
only when it resolves to an exact Core Relation or Flow transition, including
an explicitly declared inverse reading where allowed by the specification.

## Authority boundary

- `content/core.yaml` remains the locale-independent recursive semantic
  authority established by Phase 58.
- Document Description remains the complete localized prose authority and in
  v2 additionally supplies project-specific localized labels required to
  present referenced Core Steps and nodes.
- Summary Description remains the deliberate concise-content authority and in
  v2 additionally supplies coordinate-free diagram selection, retained review
  points, and explicit Document omissions.
- Cozy locale resources own generic review chrome and controlled-vocabulary
  translations.
- The renderer owns HTML, CSS, interaction, accessibility, responsive layout,
  and physical presentation.
- Generated HTML remains review evidence, never semantic authority.

## Relationship to Phase 58.1

Phase 58.1 remains closed and historically correct. Its strict v1 schemas,
identity binding, and rejection behavior remain supported. Phase 58.2 adds new
closed v2 schemas rather than weakening v1 with permissive optional fields or
silently adapting old input.

## First-slice outputs

- Phase 58.2 design and normative specification updates;
- strict typed v2 loading and validation with preserved v1 behavior;
- generic locale resources with no Article 9-specific production-code map;
- the real Article 9 v2 Document and Summary authorities;
- one deterministic interactive Document confirmation HTML;
- one deterministic interactive Summary confirmation HTML; and
- executable and visual acceptance evidence against the approved reference
  behavior.

## Deferred work

- Migration of every Document Project or scaffold/profile changes.
- SmartDox article generation and SimpleModeling.org site integration.
- Article PDF, summary-slide PDF, PPTX, infographic, video, publication,
  registration, deployment, upload, push, or external-service mutation.
- Automatic semantic inference or automatic human acceptance of AI-authored
  Core, Document, or Summary content.
