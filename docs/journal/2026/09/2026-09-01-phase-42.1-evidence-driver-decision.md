# Phase 42.1 Evidence and Isolated BoK Driver Decision

Date: 2026-09-01

Status: accepted decision record

Phase: 42.1

Decision ID: P421-DEC-001

Authority: direct user selection `A`

## Decision

Phase 42.1 may introduce a portable Document Project sidecar that binds each
declared Work Product to its receipt, review evidence, and public-source
identity. The sidecar is an evidence and driver binding; it is not a change to
the closed `cozy.document-project.v1` descriptor.

Acceptance will use an isolated BoK fixture. It does not require a live
SimpleModeling.org project or any other external repository.

## Invariants

- The closed Document Project descriptor remains unchanged.
- The existing `cozy.media.receipt.v2` receipt and review semantics remain
  unchanged. The sidecar may reference existing identities but does not
  reinterpret them.
- The eventual sidecar contract must name its product-to-evidence
  correspondence explicitly. It must not infer public identity or evidence
  correspondence from a filename or a directory layout.
- The fixture may exercise a non-Article-8 BoK-shaped project only. Article 8
  remains outside Phase 42.1.
- No site registration, workspace aggregate build, publish, deploy, upload,
  or external-project modification is authorized by this decision.

## Consequence for the Next Plan

The next Phase 42.1 planning pass will freeze the sidecar's exact name, shape,
location, validation rules, state reconstruction inputs, review projection,
and isolated-driver acceptance evidence before implementation begins.

## Deferred Work

Using a real SimpleModeling.org driver, SmartDox site registration, aggregate
BoK execution, and publication remain separate work. This decision does not
admit them into Phase 42.1.
