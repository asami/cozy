## Producer contract

The producer emits a source/model/generator envelope and typed records for
`Judgment`, `Admission`, and their `JudgmentAdmission` relation. The exact
provenance chain is:

```text
source   = literate CML text and its source locations
model    = normalized CompositeStateMachine candidate-admission IR
generator = cozy.modeler.CandidateAdmissionProducerAbiGenerator
```

Each record retains its source provenance and normalized model identity. The
envelope also retains the applicable Workflow identity, version, root,
definition source, and Required SPI correlation when those values exist in the
source model.

The `Judgment` contract carries the action identity, resolved generic
operation/input, goal, context, candidate, alternatives, criteria, expected
result, typed rationale, evidence, evidence scope, freshness, provenance, and
source location.
The `Admission` contract carries its action identity, the candidate Judgment
action it admits, the resolved operation/input, fixed local/required metadata,
and source location. `JudgmentAdmission` is the explicit typed relation between
the two records.

Required SPI correlation is descriptive producer input: it identifies the
required StateMachine SPI relationship without choosing a provider or an
execution protocol. The producer is provider-neutral. It does not encode
`ORCHESTRATION | CONTINUATION` as an Action or Participant attribute.

Lowering is performed directly from the normalized IR. Consumers receive the
typed producer ABI and must not reconstruct the meaning by reparsing CML.
