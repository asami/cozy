## Concrete external contract observations

`CozyComponentKnowledgeCarrier.requireDeclaredArchiveCarrier`. CNCF's
`src/main/scala/org/goldenport/cncf/knowledge/ComponentKnowledgeCarrierCodec.scala`
requires exactly four fields including `sha256` and constructs the typed v1
carrier from that field. This is a real consumer requirement, not permission to
retain Cozy protection or to edit CNCF here. Phase 71.5 must resolve that exact
boundary under separate consumer authority before claiming complete removal.

sbt-cozy currently supplies `runtime.cncf.descriptor.sha256` in its delegated
generation settings; Cozy's `CncfRuntimeDescriptorContract` includes digest
validation. The actual post-71.4 reader/CLI relationship needs boundary evidence
during the inventory; no downstream execution or incompatibility proof is
claimed by this source inspection alone.
