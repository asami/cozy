Follow-up: a separately scoped naming maintenance task should assess named-argument compatibility and update the declaration and affected call sites together. This baseline review does not admit that maintenance into Phase 75.1 repairs.

This nonblocking record authorizes no refactor or rename in P751-01B.

No current behavioral impact was identified. Owner: SmartDox. This nonblocking record authorizes no cleanup in P751-01B.
