## Frozen retirements and retained mixed behavior

The two Integrity digest-only scenarios `reject a non-lowercase or non-SHA-256 digest`
and `reject a digest with the wrong length`, and WipBinding's
`admit a fresh tuple and then only the exact installed WIP repeat` and
`reject a pre-existing destination for a fresh tuple without mutation`, were
retired by the preserved partial allocation. This successor retires only
InfographicEvidence's entire `reject actual build and published PNG disagreement`
scenario, which solely asserted the removed content identity.

Mixed sub-flows lose only the frozen protection rows: VideoEvidence's isolated
manifest/registry digest disagreement and stale-byte block; Policy's stale-byte
Preview/Production block and changed-video-byte registration row; BuildContext's
isolated `published-stale` row and dead digest-only fixture argument computations.
BuildContext still expects exactly registered and published-missing observations;
its five source/target/repository unsafe-path rows remain. Ordinary valid
materialization, missing/configuration/format/provenance/path/permission/I/O,
rendering, stage output and property cases remain. No replacement scenario,
absence-proof assertion, new suite or test harness was added.

WipTransaction's existing repeat scenario is now named
`refresh an existing WIP video without changing its public identity`; its actual
copy-output equality is Files.readAllBytes(...).toVector equality. Infographic
knowledge-age behavior is now `preserve PNG publication when only unrelated knowledge becomes newer`.
These amend existing ordinary behavior, without a new scenario. The old names
remain dated source history.
