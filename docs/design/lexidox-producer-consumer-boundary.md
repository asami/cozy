## Joint contract lineage and release gate

LX-J01 requires one grammar and metadata lineage, LX-J02 identity resolution before presentation, LX-J03 independent concept classification, knowledge form and display role. LX-J04–J05 add source-language, translation and profile intent in Phase 93; LX-J06 preserves canonical knowledge and RDF, LX-J07 preserves legacy behavior and requires explicit version diagnostics, and LX-J08 shares authored fixtures and tests both routes. Phase 92 accepts the semantic baseline; Phase 93 adds a compatible presentation extension and separately accepts Core/Document and rendered output. Profile selection must never re-resolve a term or alter identity, canonical knowledge, target, or prose/code. Its precedence and missing-label behavior remain P93-01 decisions.

The adopted contract is SmartDox [design and implementation plan](https://github.com/asami/smartdox/blob/7207fa191cd32bd383684ca4b278ff402ddbc096/docs/design/lexidox-producer-baseline.md) and [v1 specification](https://github.com/asami/smartdox/blob/7207fa191cd32bd383684ca4b278ff402ddbc096/docs/spec/lexidox-knowledge-space.md), source revision `7207fa191cd32bd383684ca4b278ff402ddbc096`, received through the SmartDox owner. It adopts the conceptual proposal at `25a87f783a358556ed3ddf7d97cc43a41b5efafb` and separately pins the Cozy joint plan at `8b6c7588961bb09d8c90944d384b1f960d3cff9c`. The latter maps LX-J01–LX-J08; it does not turn the uncommitted additions to the conceptual proposal into committed text. The owner reports independent review and two existing suites (11 tests) for the document task; these do not demonstrate v1 implementation.

The producer plan implements parser/profile integration, then registry, resolution, diagnostics, interchange and RDF, then builds and verifies an identified artifact. P92-02 requires its actual coordinate, source revision, portable Japanese/English and legacy fixture inputs and outputs, and producer validation receipts. These have not been delivered. Cozy retains its existing admission and SIE boundaries and must not reconstruct missing output.

The v1 contract specifies `lexidox-knowledge/1` and the semantics summarized in the handoff. Phase 93 may add optional typed fields in the same lineage while preserving v1 identities and fields; incompatible semantics require a major version. P93-01 still must decide exact syntax, mapping, profile/entry/pattern precedence, missing-label behavior and version diagnostics, and record its alignment to this decision. The shared plan establishes identity resolution before label choice and the independence of classification, form and display role, but is not evidence that P93-01 has completed. Neither public output nor producer implementation is changed by this record.
